Privacy policy

Operator: CREOS (크레오스) · Service: Salonpaper Android app and monthly subscription
Effective: October 25, 2026 · First published: October 22, 2026

Scope
This policy applies to the Salonpaper Android app (version 0.6.x) distributed through Google Play, its in-app monthly subscription (a Google Play recurring subscription), and email enquiries.

At a glance

  • There is no sign-up, and the app uses no advertising or analytics SDK.
  • Menus, photos and projects stay in the app's private storage on your device and are never sent to the operator's server.
  • The app connects over HTTPS to the operator's billing verification server (Google Cloud Run, Seoul region) only when you buy or restore a subscription, when it checks an existing subscription (on opening or returning to the app and before a paid export), or when you enter an app review code. It sends only the app identifier and the Google Play purchase token (or, for review access, the review code), and the server does not store them in a database or logs.
  • Google Play handles payments, renewals, cancellation and refunds. The operator never receives card numbers or Google passwords.
  • Support is by email only (support@getsalonpaper.com).

1. Information processed, purposes and retention

Information kept only on your device is listed separately from information processed on the operator's server or by email.

  • On-device editing data (never sent to the operator): shop name, contact details, services, prices and promotional text, resized and compressed copies of photos you choose, projects and display settings, and editing-state and error-recovery copies. Used to edit and reopen your work, and kept on your device until you delete it.
  • Subscription verification: the app identifier (package name), the Google Play purchase token, and the product, base plan, subscription status, expiry time and acknowledgement status returned by Google Play. Used to verify purchases, restores and subscription status, acknowledge valid purchases and enable exports without SAMPLE. Processed only in the server's runtime memory and not stored.
  • Subscription change notifications: the app identifier, purchase token, product, notification type and time. Used to confirm renewals, cancellations, refunds and other changes. Only notifications that have not been processed are kept in Google Pub/Sub for up to 1 day for retries.
  • Access logs: request time, path, response status, latency, IP address, user agent and similar details. Used for service operation, troubleshooting and abuse prevention, and kept in Google Cloud logs for 30 days.
  • App review access: the app identifier and the review code you enter. Used to verify exports without SAMPLE for app review. The code is kept only in the app's runtime memory, and the server holds a verification hash instead of the original code.
  • Email enquiries: your email address and the contents you send. Used to reply and resolve issues, and kept in the operator's mailbox. If you ask us to delete them, we delete them unless the law requires us to keep them.

2. Information stored on your device

So that you can edit and reopen menus and promotional images, the following is kept in the app's private storage.

  • Shop name, contact details, services, prices and promotional text you enter
  • Resized and compressed copies of photos you choose
  • Projects, display settings, editing-state copies and error-recovery copies

Photos are imported only when you pick them in the Android system picker; the app does not request access to your whole photo library or camera. Original photos are not changed. Do not enter your customers' personal information or sensitive material.

This data is not sent to the operator's server. The app has no server sync for editing data, and Android automatic backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for app data.

3. Google Play subscription verification

The monthly subscription uses Google Play Billing. The app's own internet use is limited to Google Play billing and the subscription verification described below.

  • Connection check before purchase: before opening the payment sheet, the app sends only its identifier to check that the billing verification server is ready.
  • Purchase, restore and paid-export checks: if the Google Play account on the device has an active subscription, then on purchase or restore, on opening or returning to the app, and before a paid export, the app sends the app identifier and the Google Play purchase token to the operator's HTTPS server. The server uses the Google Play Developer API to check the product, base plan, subscription status, expiry time, acknowledgement status and similar details, and acknowledges valid purchases.
  • Without a subscription: if there is no active subscription and you do not start a purchase, the app does not connect to the operator's server. Editing and exports with SAMPLE work without a server connection.

The app keeps the access result in runtime memory for up to 120 seconds and checks again for every paid export. Restarting the app or a failed check leaves no export access without SAMPLE. There is no separate app account; restores are based on purchases made by the Google Play account signed in on the device. The operator never receives card numbers or Google passwords.

Google Play handles payments, renewals, cancellation and refunds. The operator can view the order and transaction information needed for subscription support and refunds in Google Play Console. Google's processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Billing verification server, notifications and access logs

The operator uses a Google Cloud Run server in the Seoul region (asia-northeast3) and Google Pub/Sub to verify subscriptions and receive Google Play subscription change notifications. Notifications can contain the app identifier, purchase token, product, notification type and time. The server processes only notifications authenticated by Google.

  • Purchase tokens and lookup responses: processed in the server's runtime memory and not stored in a separate database, file or application log.
  • Unprocessed notifications: notifications that fail to deliver are kept in Google Pub/Sub for up to 1 day for retries. Acknowledged notifications are configured not to be retained.
  • Access logs: for service operation, troubleshooting and abuse prevention, request time, path, response status, latency, IP address, user agent and similar access details may be recorded in Google Cloud logs. The current retention setting is 30 days. Request bodies and purchase tokens are not written to application logs.
  • Rate limiting: the connecting address and request count seen by the server are processed in memory for a short time only and are not saved as a separate list.

Google Cloud is the operator's server, notification and log infrastructure. Regardless of the Seoul region setting, Google's global infrastructure may be used to run Google Play and Google Cloud services. See the Google Cloud Privacy Notice.

5. App review access

If you enter a review code provided by the operator for app review, such as Google Play review, the app sends its identifier and the code to the same HTTPS server to verify access to exports without SAMPLE only. This creates no payment or automatic renewal, and creates no account with access to menus, photos, other users' data or server administration.

The code is kept only in the app's runtime memory and is not saved to files, backups or application logs. After restarting the app, it must be entered again. The server holds a verification hash instead of the original code. Access logs are handled as in section 4, and the access result is kept for up to 120 seconds and checked again for each export.

6. When you save or share

Picking photos and files, saving PNG/PDF/JSON files and sharing images use Android system features or external apps you choose. The cloud storage or sharing apps you choose may process files under their own policies.

A JSON backup can contain the current project and imported photos. Check the contents before saving or sharing it outside the app. Files saved to the gallery or elsewhere stay in storage outside the app.

7. Email enquiries

The app's support button opens an email draft. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself. The draft includes only the app version; menus, photos and logs are never attached automatically.

The email address and contents you send (including any device model, Android version or screenshots you write or attach) are used only to reply, resolve issues and handle your request, and are kept in the operator's mailbox. To have them deleted, ask at the same address; we delete them unless the law requires us to keep them. Do not send names, phone numbers, Google account passwords, card information, purchase tokens or customer data.

The operator's support email uses Gmail, so the Google Privacy Policy also applies. The policy of the email service you use applies as well.

8. External services we use

The operator uses the services below. Each processes only the information described in the sections above.

  • Google LLC — Google Play: app distribution, subscription payments, renewals, cancellation and refunds, and subscription change notifications. Payment information that Google processes directly is governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Cloud (Cloud Run in the Seoul region, Pub/Sub, Cloud Logging): running the billing verification server, receiving subscription change notifications and keeping access logs.
  • Gmail: receiving and replying to email enquiries.

The operator does not provide personal information to third parties except as described in this policy or where required by law, and does not sell it or use it for advertising.

9. Retention and deletion

Projects stay on your device until you delete them. Editing-state and error-recovery copies may remain after you delete a project from your projects.

To remove all internal app data, first back up any JSON files you need, then use Apps → Salonpaper → Storage → Clear data in Android settings. Menu names vary by device. Uninstalling the app also deletes the data in its private storage.

Gallery images, files you saved and copies shared by email or other apps must be deleted where they are. The operator cannot view or delete data that exists only on your device.

On the server side, purchase tokens and lookup responses are not kept once processing ends. Unprocessed notifications are deleted automatically after 1 day and access logs after 30 days under the Google Cloud retention settings. Enquiry emails are deleted from the mailbox when you ask us to delete them.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel subscriptions and manage billing history in the Subscriptions menu in Google Play. Purchase and payment records held by Google follow Google's retention policies.

10. Security measures

  • Communication between the app and the billing verification server uses HTTPS only, and the app does not allow unencrypted traffic.
  • Purchase tokens are treated as secrets and are not stored or logged.
  • Subscription change notifications are accepted only when authenticated by Google.
  • Review codes are configured on the server only as a verification hash, not the original code.
  • The server runs with Google Cloud service account permissions and no separate private key file, and it limits excessive requests.
  • Editing data is kept in app-private storage that other apps cannot access, and is excluded from system automatic backup and device-transfer backup.

Please manage your device lock and access to backup files you export yourself.

11. Your rights

The app creates no separate account, so there is no app account to close. You can manage on-device data yourself as described in section 9. You can ask by email to access, correct or delete personal information the operator processes, or to stop processing it. We can act on server access logs only where the records you ask about can be identified, and please also use Google's own controls for your Google account and payment records. Do not send purchase tokens, passwords or card numbers with your request.
